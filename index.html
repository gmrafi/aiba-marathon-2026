Fair Play Policy

Tiered Prize Distribution

Prizes per category are awarded based on the number of participants in that category.

1 – 5 Participants

Only the 1st place prize shall be awarded.

6 – 9 Participants

Both the 1st and 2nd place prizes shall be awarded.

10+ Participants

1st, 2nd and 3rd place prizes shall all be awarded.

The tiered prize distribution structure does NOT apply to the Veterans and Kids categories.
